# Code of conduct

This project wants reports and criticism from anyone who has a screen or a figure: physicists and radiologists,
students and standards engineers, people who have written a great deal of code and people who have written none.
That only works if taking part is not unpleasant.

## What is expected

- Be courteous. Assume the other person is acting in good faith, and say what you mean plainly.
- Criticise the work, not the person. A figure, a paragraph of the profile or a pull request can be wrong; say
  how, and with what evidence.
- Accept that people know different things. A question that seems basic to you is not a fault in the person
  asking it.
- Say when you are unsure, and when you were wrong.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or remarks that demean a person for who they are.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Continuing after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere else a person is acting for
the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer, [@Thenewmanator15](https://github.com/Thenewmanator15), or use GitHub's own
[report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
route, which does not go through the project. Reports are kept private. The maintainer may edit or remove
contributions that break this code, and may bar a person from the project's spaces.

## Standard

Where this text is silent, the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
is the standard applied.
