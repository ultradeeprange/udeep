Move the knee and watch

Here the mapping from data to light runs on your graphics card, for every pixel, on every frame. Change the knee, the stretch or the headroom and the picture follows at once. On an HDR screen the values above the knee are brighter than white; on any other screen they are white.

This is the profile's reference shader, the same one that is tested against the project's test vectors. The data on this page is synthetic, made in your browser. You can also open an array of your own; it stays on your computer.
