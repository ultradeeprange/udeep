Device checks

Test pages from the profile's Annex A. Each has a control that must not glow and a positive control that must. If a control fails, the result doesn't count.

Record your device, OS and browser versions, brightness setting and room light with your result. Remote-desktop sessions deliver SDR, so don't use them.

Which HDR formats does this screen show?

One referenced pattern in gain-map JPEG, HDR PNG, AVIF and HDR video.

Does HDR survive inside SVG?

Inline, linked and standalone SVG; vector colours above white.

Does 12-bit HDR video play?

HEVC and AV1 at 10 and 12 bits.

Which strips have bands?

A blind banding test: PQ ramps rounded to 8 to 16 bits.
